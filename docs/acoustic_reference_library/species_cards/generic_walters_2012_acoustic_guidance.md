# Generic Acoustic Guidance From Walters et al. 2012

This card is a compact, model-facing acoustic checklist grounded in Walters et al. 2012. It is not a species card and must not be used as an `Ozimops petersi` prior.

## Model-Facing Checklist

- Identify the visible onset and offset of each coherent bat-call ridge.
- Estimate lower and upper frequency bounds from the visible signal, not from background texture.
- Consider duration and bandwidth as consistency checks for the box extent.
- Inspect peak or centre frequency and the main frequency-time ridge shape.
- Notice whether the ridge is steep, shallow, curved, quasi-constant-frequency, or frequency-modulated.
- Avoid boxing background noise, echoes, overload, isolated artefacts, or unrelated spectrogram energy.
- Flag weak, noisy, truncated, or ambiguous evidence for review rather than forcing a confident annotation.
- Do not force any species-specific numeric range.
- Do not transfer European bat species ranges, call durations, or frequencies to `Ozimops petersi`.

## Caution

Walters et al. 2012 concerns European bat acoustic identification. In this project it is only a generic acoustic-parameter and quality-control reference for localisation. The spectrogram remains the primary evidence for each annotation.
